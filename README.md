**Himanshu Singh**, Steven J. Greybush, Laura E. Dailey, Melissa Adrian, and Romit Maulik

**40th Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2026)**

A matrix-free framework for learning and evaluating covariance response in generative weather ensembles, motivated by the requirements of ensemble-based data assimilation.

[📄 Paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=HQvUxeF3jA)

---

### Operator-Adapted Reproducing Kernel Geometries for Structure-Preserving Representation Learning

**Himanshu Singh**

**40th Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2026)**

Develops an inverse geometric formulation of representation learning: rather than modifying an operator to preserve a prescribed geometry, construct an RKHS geometry in which the given operator acts isometrically. The work characterizes the complete family of admissible invariant kernels and develops operator-adapted Gaussian representations.

[📄 Paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=wBN8kwEGN8)

---

### Kernelized Koopman Representation Geometry Governs Transient Amplification in Neural Dynamical Systems

**Himanshu Singh**

**NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Symmetry and Geometry in Neural Representations — Proceedings Track**

Studies how kernel-induced representation geometry governs transient amplification in neural dynamical systems through a Koopman-operator perspective.

[📄 Paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=ZKhFhmuyuq)

**Himanshu Singh**

**Nishant Panda, Himanshu Singh, and J. Nathan Kutz**

**arXiv:2505.06806, 2025**

Studies the operator-theoretic foundations of kernel Dynamic Mode Decomposition by constructing a Laplacian-kernel RKHS and investigating closability of Koopman operators, with applications to sparse spatiotemporal reconstruction.

**Himanshu Singh**

***Mathematics*, 12(6), 829, 2024**
